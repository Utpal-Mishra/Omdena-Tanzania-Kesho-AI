# AI-Powered Early Warning System for Extreme Weather in Tanzania

This repository contains the details and outcomes of the grassroots AI Climathon event hosted by Omdena in partnership with EIT Climate-KIC and Sahara Ventures. The event focused on the development of a precision early warning system for predicting extreme weather conditions in Tanzania.

## Summary
We are building a 24-hour AI-powered precision early warning system designed to predict and provide timely alerts for extreme weather conditions in Tanzania. This system will utilize data from the Tanzania Meteorological Authority (TMA) and historical data to predict floods, heavy rains, and heat waves. It will deliver tailored alerts via SMS to individuals, enhancing preparedness and response to weather-related disasters.

## Problem Statement
**Why are we doing this?**
Unpredictable weather conditions caused by climate change lead to severe adverse effects such as floods, heat waves, and other extreme weather events in Tanzania. These conditions result in significant property damage, displacements, and fatalities. There is a lack of tailored preparedness measures, specific location pinpointing, and timely alerts, leading to inadequate disaster response and low accessibility of vital information.

## Goal
### SMART: Specific, Measurable, Attainable, Relevant, and Time-bound

- **Specific:** Develop a robust AI-powered early warning system to predict extreme weather conditions in Tanzania, specifically floods, heavy rains, and heat waves. The system will leverage data from TMA and historical weather data to provide accurate alerts via SMS.

- **Measurable:**
  1. Achieve a prediction accuracy of at least 80% for floods, heavy rains, and heat waves within the first eight weeks of model development.
  2. Develop and deploy a user-friendly dashboard visualising weather predictions and system performance.

- **Achievable:** Utilize existing machine learning frameworks and pre-trained models for weather prediction. Collaborate with TMA to gain access to historical weather data and leverage the expertise of Omdena's global network of AI specialists and data scientists.

- **Relevant:** This project addresses the critical need for timely and accurate weather information in Tanzania, which is prone to extreme weather events due to climate change. The system will help save lives, reduce property damage, and improve disaster responsiveness by providing early warnings.

- **Time-Bound:** Complete the development and initial deployment of the Kesho AI system within 8 + 2 weeks.

## Approach
The project will utilize data-driven methodologies and machine-learning techniques to analyze historical weather data and develop predictive models. These models will be integrated into an early warning system communicating risks to residents through SMS alerts.

## Implementation
- Collaborate with the Tanzania Meteorological Authority to acquire necessary data.
- Build predictive models using historical weather data and real-time inputs.
- Create a dashboard for visualizing weather predictions and system performance.
- Develop a communication system for delivering alerts via SMS.

## Metrics for Success
- Model accuracy and performance in predicting extreme weather events.
- User engagement with the dashboard and alert systems.
- Feedback from local communities on the effectiveness of the warnings provided.

## Future Work
Further enhancements to the system can include:
- Expanding the predictive capabilities to include additional weather phenomena.
- Integrating community feedback to refine alert systems and user engagement.
- Collaborating with local authorities to broaden the dissemination of alerts.
